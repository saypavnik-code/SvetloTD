## Method and limits

- The harness of `docs/ECONOMY_REPORT.md` with real `Seats`, `BuilderManager` and `BuildSystem`: every seat has its own wallet (the starting gold is
  split without loss), buys only in its own zones and runs the same build rules on its own gold. Every seat owns every builder, so the
  numbers isolate what the zones and the split wallets do. Idle hero on the base, normal difficulty, 24 cells, no selling.
- Layout `coverage` is the old list: the 24 cells that cover the most lane waypoints. Layout `zones` takes the 6 best cells of the north-west
  zone and turns them by 90 degrees into the other three zones (the map is rotation-symmetric), interleaved by rank, so 1, 2 and 4 seats use
  exactly the same cells and only the wallets and the zone rule differ.
- A build is a strict-priority rule list, so one seat can wait for an expensive upgrade while another buys: differences between seat counts
  partly measure the rule lists, not the game. They are a guard against a handicap for co-operation, not a balance target.
